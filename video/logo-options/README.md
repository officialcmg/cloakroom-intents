# Logo mark options

Ten marks for the rounded-square tile (32x32 viewBox, white mark on the green tile). `board.png` shows each one on white, on deep green, reversed, in the header lock-up and at 32 px and 16 px.

1. **Claim Tag** (`option-01.svg`): A coat-check tag with its punched hole and two notches. You hand over the coat and keep the tag: the claim.
2. **Torn Stub** (`option-02.svg`): A cloakroom ticket torn into ticket and stub. Two halves of one claim: the protocol splits the claim key the same way.
3. **Quiet Hood** (`option-03.svg`): A hood reduced to one pointed arch with an empty face. The cloak in the name: the person is there, the identity is not.
4. **Closed C** (`option-04.svg`): A heavy C around a single point: the C of Cloakroom, and a coin held inside a closed hand. Reads as "Ci" at a glance.
5. **Shield Routes** (`option-05.svg`): A shield on its side, its point opening into three routes that end at three chains. One shielded source, many places to land.
6. **Drawn Curtain** (`option-06.svg`): A doorway whose curtain is drawn open and tied back. The cloakroom counter, and a view that only opens when you choose.
7. **Wrapped Coin** (`option-07.svg`): A coin with a band wrapped across it. The coin is still there and still whole; it is just covered while it moves.
8. **Open Cape** (`option-08.svg`): A cape seen from the front, held by one clasp and open at the hem. The cloak, without the hanger.
9. **Split Seal** (`option-09.svg`): A round seal with a keyhole, cut in two and slightly offset. Line the halves up and the keyhole opens: the claim.
10. **Domino Mask** (`option-10.svg`): A plain eye mask in two curves. Private by default, the same way the shielded pool is.
