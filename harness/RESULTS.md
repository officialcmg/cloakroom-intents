### s25_maker_vanishes_after_claim

The maker process is killed the moment the taker's claim is final. The taker's key sends withdraw(id) from what the stipend left and ends with the tokens under its key in the Verifier, with no other party. On local and testnet networks the exit goes through the maker's relay, so with the maker gone the scenario ends at the tokens in the Verifier. The maker returns afterwards and sweeps the ZEC
